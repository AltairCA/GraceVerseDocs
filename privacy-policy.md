## Summary

GraceVerse is a Catholic Scripture companion: daily verse, mood-based verse matching, chapter reading, and optional home-screen widgets. **No account is required.**

- **On your device:** we store a short cache of daily verses, local Bible bookmarks/recents, and simple preferences (for example language).
- **On our servers:** the App requests Scripture content and mood matches from the GraceVerse API over HTTPS. We do **not** create a user profile or require sign-in.
- We do **not** sell your personal information, show third-party ads, or use advertising trackers in the current version.

---

## 1. Information the App Stores on Your Device

The App may store the following locally on your phone or tablet (for example via device app storage / AsyncStorage):

| Data type | Purpose | Stored where |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Daily verse window (and chapter context when included) | Show today’s verse offline and power widgets | App storage on device |
| Bible bookmarks and recent chapters | Let you reopen places you saved or visited | App storage on device |
| Language / display preferences (if set) | Request content in your preferred language | App storage on device |
| Temporary UI state | Remember last screens within a session | App memory / storage on device |

This local data is **not synced to a GraceVerse user account** (there is no account system in the current version). Uninstalling the App or clearing app data removes it, subject to your operating system.

---

## 2. Information Sent to the GraceVerse API

To provide Scripture and matching features, the App communicates with our API (currently hosted at `graceverse.altairsl.us`) over **HTTPS**. Depending on what you do, requests may include:

| Data | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Language code (e.g. `en`) | Daily verse, moods, Bible browse/search | Return content in the requested language |
| Date / day range | Daily verse prefetch | Fetch the verse schedule for those days |
| Mood slug and intensity level | Mood match | Return a fitting curated verse |
| Bible book, chapter, reference, or search keywords | Bible reader / search | Return chapter text or search hits |

We use this information to answer your request. We do **not** use it to build a marketing profile or personalize ads.

Our servers and hosting provider may also process ordinary technical data needed to run a website/API (for example IP address, timestamps, user-agent, and request path) in server or access logs, for security, reliability, and abuse prevention. Retention of such logs depends on our hosting configuration and is kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes.

If you choose **Donate** in the App, we open PayPal in your browser or the PayPal app. Payment details are handled by **PayPal**, not by GraceVerse. See PayPal’s privacy policy: https://www.paypal.com/privacy

## 4. Device Permissions

The current App is designed to work without camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location access.

Home-screen **widgets** (where available) read the locally cached daily verse on your device so the widget can show today’s Scripture. Widget behavior follows your operating system’s rules.

If a future version requests additional permissions, we will update this policy and the store listing.

---

## 5. What We Do **Not** Collect

In the current version of the App, we do **not**:

- Require you to create an account or provide an email to use core features
- Sell your personal information
- Show third-party advertising in the App
- Use advertising SDKs to track you across other apps
- Collect precise location data
- Access your contacts, calendar, SMS, camera, or microphone

## 6. Children’s Privacy

GraceVerse offers Scripture content suitable for a general audience but is **not directed at children under 13** (or the minimum age required in your country) as a children’s product. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for accounts (we do not offer accounts). If you believe a child has data stored only on a device, clearing app data or uninstalling the App removes local storage.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

**On your device:** local cache, bookmarks, and preferences remain until you clear them in the App (where available), clear app storage in system settings, or uninstall the App.

**On our servers:** API requests are processed to return Scripture content. We do not maintain a per-user content library tied to your identity. Technical logs are retained only as reasonably needed for security and operations.

---

## 8. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a mobile app and HTTPS API, including:

- Transport encryption (HTTPS) between the App and the GraceVerse API
- Keeping local app data in the app’s private sandbox on your device

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device secure.
